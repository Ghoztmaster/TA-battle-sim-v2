# Provenance and license status

This is a maintained fork of Tiberium Alliances Battle Simulator V2 (TABS).

**Original authors:** Eistee, TheStriker, VisiG, Lobotommi, XDaast (2013–2019), repository `Eistee82/ta_simv2`.
**Later maintained by:** NetquiK [SoO] (2019–2023), repository `netquik/CnCTA-SoO-SCRIPT-PACK`. Last upstream version: 23.05.31.
**This fork:** Ghozt [SoO], from October 2026. Starting point is upstream 23.05.31, unchanged.

**License:** the original authors published TABS without a license. We have not obtained one and do not claim one. This fork exists to keep the script working for the Tiberium Alliances community after upstream maintenance stopped. If you are one of the original authors and object to this fork, open an issue at https://github.com/Ghoztmaster/TA-battle-sim-v2/issues or contact Ghozt on the TA forum and it will be taken down.

NetquiK's repository carries a GPL-3.0 notice; that covers his own modifications only, not the original code. We do not re-license anything.
